Why small runs

Fewer pieces, made properly

We cut each style in a limited run rather than restocking endlessly — it means better fabric, a tighter fit, and fewer pieces that end up unsold at season's end.

Members get first access because they're the ones who've told us what to make more of. It's less a loyalty program than an actual feedback loop.
